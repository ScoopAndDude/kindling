HAUNTINGS OF THE WORLD

Places the Past Still Walks

A curated guide to Earth’s most compelling haunted locations. Real history, documented reports, and the stories that refuse to stay buried.

Europe

Tower of London

London, England

Nearly a thousand years of imprisonment and execution. The headless ghost of Anne Boleyn is frequently reported leading silent processions, alongside the lost Princes in the Tower.

Americas

The Stanley Hotel

Estes Park, Colorado

Inspiration for Stephen King’s The Shining. Room 217 (site of a 1911 explosion) and the ghosts of founders F.O. and Flora Stanley are among the most reported apparitions.

Europe

Paris Catacombs

Paris, France

The remains of more than six million people arranged in elaborate patterns. Visitors report cold spots, whispers, and an overwhelming sense of the surrounding dead.

Europe

Edinburgh Castle

Edinburgh, Scotland

Perched on volcanic rock, its dungeons once held soldiers and civilians. Ghosts of prisoners, a spectral piper, and a dog in the cemetery are commonly reported.

Europe

Poveglia Island

Venice Lagoon, Italy

Once a plague quarantine station, later an asylum. Locals avoid it; reports of tormented spirits and a mad doctor’s ghost persist despite historical debate on the death toll.

Asia

Bhangarh Fort

Rajasthan, India

India’s most haunted fort, cursed so no one may live within its walls after sunset. Official ASI signs warn visitors to leave before dark due to local belief and safety.

Americas

Island of the Dolls

Xochimilco, Mexico

Hundreds of decaying dolls hang from trees, placed by caretaker Don Julián Santana to appease the spirit of a drowned girl. He himself was later found drowned nearby.

Americas

Winchester Mystery House

San Jose, California

Built by Sarah Winchester to confuse spirits of those killed by Winchester rifles. Staircases to nowhere, doors that open onto walls, and continuous construction for 38 years.

Europe

Leap Castle

County Offaly, Ireland

Often called Ireland’s most haunted castle. Home to the “Red Lady,” an elemental spirit, and a bloody history of family betrayal and murder spanning centuries.

Europe

Corvin Castle

Hunedoara, Romania

Gothic fortress once holding Vlad the Impaler. Visitors report cold drafts, voices in empty chambers, and the lingering presence of its most infamous prisoner.

Americas

Lizzie Borden House

Fall River, Massachusetts

Site of the unsolved 1892 axe murders of Andrew and Abby Borden. Overnight guests report footsteps, cold spots, and the sound of an axe.

Americas

Eastern State Penitentiary

Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

Once the most famous prison in the world, known for solitary confinement. Now a ruin filled with reports of cell-block apparitions, whispers, and residual energy.

Asia

Aokigahara Forest

Mount Fuji, Japan

The “Sea of Trees” — dense, silent woodland associated with tragic endings. Visitors describe an oppressive atmosphere where sound seems to vanish.

Europe

Hoia-Baciu Forest

Cluj-Napoca, Romania

Twisted trees and a circular clearing where nothing grows. Legends of disappearances, UFO photos from 1968, and the “Bermuda Triangle of Transylvania.”

Europe

Čachtice Castle

Slovakia

Home of Countess Elizabeth Báthory, accused of torturing and killing hundreds of young women. The ruins still carry the weight of her legend.

Americas

Amityville Horror House

Amityville, New York

Site of the 1974 DeFeo family murders, later claimed by the Lutz family to be the source of intense paranormal activity that inspired books and films.

Stories That Linger

Anne Boleyn’s Procession

In the 19th century, a soldier at the Tower of London reported a blue-white light in the Chapel of St Peter ad Vincula. Peering inside, he saw a silent procession of Tudor-dressed figures led by the headless Anne Boleyn. Other accounts describe her carrying her severed head under her arm across Tower Green.

The Nightmare That Became The Shining

In September 1974, Stephen King and his wife were the only guests at the Stanley Hotel as it closed for winter. That night, King dreamed his young son was chased down the corridors by a living fire hose. By morning, the outline of the novel was complete. They stayed in Room 217, site of an earlier gas explosion that injured chambermaid Elizabeth Wilson.

The Curse of Bhangarh

According to legend, a wizard cursed the fort after the king’s daughter rejected his advances. The curse stipulated that the fort would never be inhabited again. Today, official signs warn visitors to leave before sunset, and many locals refuse to enter after dark.

The Red Lady of Leap Castle

One of Leap Castle’s most persistent spirits is the Red Lady, said to be a woman murdered by her own family. An elemental entity is also reported — a dark, malevolent presence that has allegedly driven visitors from the castle in fear.

Visiting these places: Most are open to the public. Respect local customs, check access rules (especially after dark), and remember that the supernatural remains a matter of personal belief. Experiences vary widely.
