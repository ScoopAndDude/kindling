Help Cambo’s family get a roof and heat before winter

Cambo’s wife, Luna, needs her roof fixed and a woodstove hooked up before winter sets in at their home in Chapmansboro, Tennessee. Cambo can’t be there to do it right now, so I’m asking you.

Give on Luna’s GoFundMe Share this page

Cambo’s family needs a hand

Cambo is my best friend, and he’s family to me. Some of you know him from my TikTok videos.

Why it can’t wait

Lows in Chapmansboro are forecast between 29 and 33 degrees through Tuesday. It was about 34 degrees there early this morning, Saturday, Nov. 14.

There’s one break coming. Wednesday through Saturday, Nov. 18 to 21, look dry, with highs in the upper 60s to low 70s. That’s the window to get the roof done and the stove in. After that, it’s late November in Middle Tennessee, and the cold nights come right back.

What she needs

  • Roof repairs she can’t cover on her own.
  • A woodstove hooked up the right way, with the correct stovepipe, safe clearances and a proper pass-through at the wall or roof. Done wrong, a stove can start a fire or leak carbon monoxide, so this needs someone who knows what they’re doing.
  • Local help: a roofer with a fair price, a free estimate, spare roofing materials or stovepipe parts.
